Banana Bread Recipe

Ingredients:
- 3 ripe bananas
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 1 tsp baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1.5 cups flour

Instructions:
Preheat oven to 175C. Mix mashed bananas with melted butter.
Mix in baking soda and salt. Stir in sugar, egg, and vanilla.
Add flour and mix until just combined. Pour into a loaf pan
and bake for 60 minutes.
