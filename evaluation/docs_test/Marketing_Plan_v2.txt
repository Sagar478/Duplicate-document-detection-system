Marketing Plan - Spring Campaign

Objective: Increase brand awareness among university students in Copenhagen.

Strategy:
- Run social media ads on Instagram and TikTok.
- Partner with student influencers for product reviews.
- Offer a 15% discount code for first-time buyers.
- Host a pop-up stand at campus events.

Budget: 7000 DKK
Timeline: 6 weeks starting April 1st.
